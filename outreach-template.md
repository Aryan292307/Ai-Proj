# Client Outreach Template

Use this as a starting script — tweak the business name/type each time, keep it short.

## Cold email / DM

Subject: Quick website idea for [Business Name]

Hi [Owner's name],

I noticed [Business Name] doesn't have a website / your current site looks outdated. I build fast, simple websites for local businesses — here's an example I made for a [salon/café/tutor]: [link to matching sample site].

I'd like to build you a free mockup of a homepage, no obligation. If you like it, we can talk about going live for a small fee.

Would that be useful?

[Your name]
[Your email]

## In-person pitch (30 seconds)

"Hi, I build websites for local businesses like yours. I can put together a free sample homepage for you this week — if you like it, we talk price. If not, no cost either way. Want me to take a shot at it?"

## Follow-up (if no reply after ~5 days)

Hi [Owner's name], just following up on the free website mockup I offered — still happy to put one together if you're interested. Low effort on your end either way.

## Pricing ladder (raise as you gain proof of work)
1. First 2-3 clients: $30-75 (build portfolio + testimonials)
2. Next clients: $100-250 per site
3. Once you have 5+ live sites: $250-500+, or recurring maintenance fee ($10-20/month)
