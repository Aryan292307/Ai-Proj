Frequently asked

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments get priority. Weekends fill up fast, so book 2-3 days ahead if you can.

How long does a color service take?

Single-process color: about 1 hour. Balayage or full highlights: 2.5-3.5 hours depending on hair length.

Do you do home service for brides?

Yes, for bridal packages booked at least 2 weeks in advance. Travel charges apply outside city limits.
